Privacy Policy

Sapthagiri School Portal

Effective date: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sapthagiri High School E/M ("School", "we", "us" or "our") handles personal information in connection with the Sapthagiri School Portal and related services, including the portal's WhatsApp Business integration.

1. Who we are

The Sapthagiri School Portal is an administrative and communication platform used by Sapthagiri High School E/M. The portal supports school administration and communication functions such as student management, attendance, examinations, fees, staff management and related school operations.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at sapthagiri.98@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

Depending on how the portal is used, we may process information necessary to operate school services, including:

  • Student information maintained by the School for educational and administrative purposes.
  • Parent or guardian contact information where provided to the School.
  • Staff and administrator account information.
  • Login, authentication and account-related information.
  • Attendance, examination, academic, fee and other school-management records entered into the portal.
  • Contact information used for school communications.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure and troubleshoot the portal.

3. WhatsApp Business integration

The portal can connect the School's WhatsApp Business account through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. This integration is used by authorized school personnel to facilitate school communications.

When the WhatsApp integration is connected, the portal may process information associated with the connected WhatsApp Business account, such as the business account identifier, WhatsApp Business Account identifier, phone number identifier, business phone number and messaging-related information required to provide the integration.

WhatsApp messages and related information are processed to provide the communication functionality requested by the School. We do not use WhatsApp information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the School Portal.
  • Manage authorized user accounts and access.
  • Maintain school administrative and academic records.
  • Communicate with students, parents, guardians and staff where appropriate.
  • Send or receive school communications through the connected WhatsApp Business account.
  • Protect the portal against unauthorized access, misuse and security threats.
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain system reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal and administrative obligations.

5. Legal and educational context

The School processes information primarily in connection with its educational, administrative and communication responsibilities. Access to student and staff information is limited according to the roles and permissions implemented by the School Portal.

Where the portal processes information relating to students, including children, such information is handled for legitimate school and educational purposes and according to the School's responsibilities toward students and their parents or guardians.

6. Sharing of information

We may disclose information where necessary to operate the portal, provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, protect the security of the School or its users, or as otherwise authorized by the School.

The WhatsApp integration uses services provided by Meta Platforms, including the WhatsApp Business Platform. Information transmitted through that integration is subject to the applicable Meta and WhatsApp terms and policies.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Meta and WhatsApp

The portal's WhatsApp integration uses Meta's services to connect the School's WhatsApp Business account and facilitate messaging. Meta may process information according to its own policies and the permissions granted to the School's WhatsApp Business integration.

Users should also review Meta's applicable privacy information for details about how Meta processes information on its services.

8. Data retention

School records and account information may be retained for as long as necessary for educational, administrative, operational, security or legal purposes. Retention periods may therefore differ depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is held.

When information is no longer required for the relevant purpose, the School may delete, anonymize or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable record-retention requirements.

9. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials and should not share passwords or access tokens with unauthorized persons.

10. Access and correction

Students, parents or guardians, staff and other authorized users may contact the School regarding personal information held about them. Requests for access, correction or other appropriate action will be handled according to the School's applicable procedures and legal obligations.

11. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of personal information, or to ask about the deletion of information associated with the WhatsApp integration, contact:

Email: sapthagiri.98@gmail.com

Please include enough information for the School to identify the relevant account or record. We may need to verify the identity and authority of the person making a request before taking action.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, educational, accounting, security or other legitimate record-keeping purposes.

12. Cookies and local storage

The School Portal may use browser storage, cookies or similar technical mechanisms where required for authentication, session management, preferences, security or normal operation of the portal.

13. Third-party services

The portal may rely on third-party infrastructure and services to provide hosting, authentication, database, messaging or other technical functionality. Such services receive only the information necessary for the relevant service, subject to their applicable terms and privacy requirements.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the School Portal, its integrations or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
